Dev panel

Shift lights
LED turn-on RPM
Drag a point (or the red flash line) up or down

Each LED turns on at or above its RPM. At or above the flash RPM the whole bar flashes. RPMs can't go down from one LED to the next.

Auto-fill

Fills the 16 LED RPMs in the table. Nothing is sent until you press Save.

Preview

Uses the mode, brightness and RPMs above, before you save them. On the car, unsaved changes are undone when you turn the preview off (or if the connection drops). Real RPM is ignored while previewing.
